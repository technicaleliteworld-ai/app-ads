Account Deletion Request

If you wish to delete your MomWell account and all associated data, you have two options:

Option 1: Delete from within the App (Recommended)

  • Open the MomWell app on your device.
  • Go to the Settings menu.
  • Scroll down to the "Account Actions" section.
  • Tap on Sign Out / Delete Account.
  • Your account and all associated health tracking data will be permanently removed from our servers.

Option 2: Request via Email

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request data deletion by emailing us directly:

  • Send an email to: support@kmsteams.com
  • Subject: "Account Deletion Request"
  • Please include the email address associated with your MomWell account in the body of the email.
  • We will process your request and delete all your data within 7 business days.

Note: Once deleted, your data (including pregnancy logs, medicine trackers, and chat history) cannot be recovered.
