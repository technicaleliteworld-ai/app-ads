Bandhan App - Account Deletion Request

We are sorry to see you go. If you no longer wish to use the Bandhan Matrimony app and want to delete your account and associated personal data, you can do so easily by following the steps below.

Method 1: Delete Account from inside the App

The fastest way to delete your account is directly through the app:

  1. Open the Bandhan app and log in to your account.
  2. Go to the Menu / Profile section.
  3. Navigate to Settings.
  4. Tap on Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your choice. Your account and data will be permanently deleted immediately.

Method 2: Request Deletion via Email

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, you can request data deletion via email:

  1. Send an email to aidevgen@gmail.com from the email address registered with your account.
  2. Set the subject line to: "Request for Account Deletion".
  3. Include your registered Phone Number or Email ID in the email body so we can verify and locate your account.
  4. Our team will process your request and permanently delete your data within 7-14 business days.

What data will be deleted?

When your account is deleted, the following data will be permanently removed from our active servers:

  • Your public profile information (Name, Bio, Preferences).
  • All uploaded photos and media.
  • Your chat history and connections with other users.
  • Your contact information (Email, Phone number).

Data Retention

Please note that we may retain certain data (such as transaction records for premium subscriptions) for legal, tax, or regulatory compliance purposes even after your account is deleted.
