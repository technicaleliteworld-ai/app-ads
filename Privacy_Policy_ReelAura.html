Welcome to ReelAura: AI Reels & Story Maker ("we", "our", or "us"), developed and published by VidoStatus. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our users.

By downloading, accessing, or using ReelAura, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

A. User-Provided Photos & Media (AI Generation)

When using features such as AI Photo Templates, Virtual Try-On, or AI Video Reels, you may upload photos from your device gallery or capture live photos with your camera.

🔒 Strict Privacy Guarantee: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal photos. Uploaded media is securely transmitted using encrypted HTTPS/TLS 1.3 tunnels to dedicated cloud processing servers solely to fulfill your generation request. Source photos are treated ephemerally and are automatically purged from processing servers after generation. Your photos are never used to train public AI models.

B. Device & Diagnostic Data

  • Device Specifications: Device model, operating system version (iOS or Android), app version, and language/locale.
  • Anonymous Device Identifier: A securely hashed device identifier generated on launch. This allows guest access and maintains credits/VIP plan entitlements without requiring social login or password-based accounts.
  • Crash & Performance Logs: Anonymized crash logs and telemetry via Firebase Crashlytics to detect and fix app crashes.

C. In-App Purchases & Billing

  • Apple App Store (iOS): All in-app purchases, subscriptions (Weekly Pro, Annual VIP), and coin packs are processed directly and securely by Apple via Apple In-App Purchases (StoreKit).
  • Google Play Store (Android): All transactions are processed directly and securely through Google Play In-App Billing.
  • We never collect, store, or have access to your credit card, debit card, or sensitive financial payment details.

D. Analytics & Advertising

  • Analytics: We use anonymized analytics (Firebase Analytics) to track aggregate feature usage and improve app responsiveness.
  • Advertising (Free Tier): Non-paying users may see advertisements powered by Google AdMob and Apple SKAdNetwork. Paid VIP users enjoy a 100% ad-free experience.
