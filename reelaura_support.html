💡 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I create an AI Reel or Story?
Open ReelAura, tap "AI Video Reels" on the home dashboard or pick one of the quick presets. Enter your topic or prompt, choose your desired voice and language, tap "Generate Script", and then tap "Render Reel". Your multi-scene video will be rendered and saved to your device.
2. How does the Virtual Fashion Try-On work?
Navigate to Fashion Studio, pick an outfit or saree style from the catalogue, and upload a clear, front-facing portrait of yourself. Our AI will photorealistically align the fabric, lighting, and drape onto your photo.
3. How do I restore my VIP Purchases or Subscriptions?
If you reinstalled the app or switched devices:
  1. Open ReelAura.
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon).
  3. Tap "Restore Purchases".
Your active Apple App Store or Google Play subscription will be validated and your VIP status will be restored immediately.
4. How do free trial credits work?
Every new installation receives complimentary free generation credits so you can experience ReelAura's features without payment. You can also earn extra bonus credits through daily check-ins, rewarded videos, or by upgrading to a VIP plan.
5. What should I do if an AI generation fails or gets stuck?
Generations rely on high-performance cloud AI GPUs. If your connection drops or an error occurs:
  • Check your internet connection.
  • Go to Settings > Storage & Cache and tap "Clear Cache".
  • If your quota was deducted without delivering a result, please email us with your Device ID (found at the bottom of Settings), and we will refund your credits.
6. How do I request account or data deletion?
emailing us at support@cscodetech.cloud.
