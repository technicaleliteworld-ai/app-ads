Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access premium chapters?
You can unlock premium chapters by becoming a Premium Subscriber, purchasing coin packs to unlock individual chapters, or using our daily reward bonuses.
How do I restore my purchases?
Go to the "Go Premium" screen in the app, and tap on "Restore Purchases" at the bottom of the screen. This will sync any active App Store subscriptions with your account.
Can I read novels offline?
Yes! You can download chapters of your favorite web novels or light novels to your library, allowing you to read them anywhere without an internet connection.
