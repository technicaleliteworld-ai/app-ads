Child Safety Standards and Policy

Effective Date: July 03, 2026

Welcome to the Bandhan Matrimony App. We are strictly committed to ensuring the safety of children and maintaining a secure platform. Our application is designed exclusively for adults seeking a life partner.

1. Age Restriction (Strictly 18+)

The Bandhan app is strictly restricted to users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not allow minors (children under 18) to create an account, use our services, or be featured on our platform.

2. Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)

We have a zero-tolerance policy against any form of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) or Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). It is strictly prohibited to upload, share, or promote any content that exploits or harms children.

3. User Reporting and Moderation

We empower our users to maintain community safety. Inside the Bandhan app, users can immediately Report or Block any suspicious account, behavior, or content. If any content involving child exploitation is reported or detected by our moderation team, we will:

  • Immediately terminate and ban the user's account.
  • Preserve the related data.
  • Report the user and the incident to the relevant regional and national law enforcement authorities.

4. Compliance with Laws

We comply with all relevant child safety laws. We actively cooperate with law enforcement and non-governmental organizations to combat child exploitation and abuse globally.
